KhomaVolume NQ Validation

One rule survived 26,883 strategy tests across 25 Lucid-tradable futures: at 10:30 ET, trade MNQ in the direction of the 9:30→10:30 move when it agrees with the 20-day trend, stop at 0.3× the daily range, exit at 16:00. This page shows its 10-year record, the stress tests it went through, the Lucid presets and a simulated forward test.

Read this first

The max-level audit found a look-ahead bias in the earlier multi-signal "trade every day" version: it made money on a pure random walk. That version is retired. Every number on this page uses the single 10:30 rule, which loses only its costs on random data. Honest pass speed is about two weeks, not six days.

What this proves and what it doesn't

Proven

Positive expectancy 2017–2025, unbiased simulator, robust to costs, noise, latency, missed trades and parameter changes, and profitable when re-fitted blind each year.

Not proven

That it is working right now. The last 12 months are flat (PF about 1.0). If this regime continues, expect results near the luck baseline.

What clients should hear

About 2 in 5 evaluations pass, typically in 2–3 weeks. Funded payouts take about 3–5 months. Run accounts one after another or staggered, never as simultaneous copies.

What to do first

Make order execution reliable within a minute of 10:30, and track live results against this page every month.
