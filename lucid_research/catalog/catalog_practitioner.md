# Practitioner / Retail / Prop-Firm Strategy Catalog

Scope: intraday futures setups from the practitioner world (TradingView, YouTube, Reddit, NexusFi/futures.io, Elite Trader, BabyPips/ForexFactory-style forums, prop-firm and vendor blogs, ICT/SMC, Al Brooks, Market Profile, VWAP, indicator and pivot systems). Compiled 2026-10-11.

Conventions used in the rule column (all times US/Eastern; every strategy is force-flat by 16:45 ET unless stated earlier):
- `Idx` = ES, NQ, RTY, YM (+ MES/MNQ/M2K/MYM). `Met` = GC, SI, HG, PL (+ MGC). `En` = CL, NG (+ MCL). `FX` = 6A/6B/6E/6J/6S/6N. `Ag` = ZC/ZS/ZW/ZL/ZM. `Liv` = HE/LE. `All` = every Lucid product.
- `RTH` = 09:30–16:00 for index futures; product "pit" opens: CL/NG 09:00, GC/SI/HG 08:20, grains 09:30 (08:30 CT), 6E/FX 08:20, livestock 09:30.
- `OR(n)` = high/low of the first n minutes after the product's session open. `ORH/ORL/ORM` = high/low/mid. `IB` = 09:30–10:30 range. `PDH/PDL/PDC` = prior RTH high/low/close. `ONH/ONL` = Globex overnight (18:00–09:29) high/low. `VWAP` = session VWAP anchored at 18:00 (or 09:30 for "RTH VWAP"). `ATR14d` = 14-day ATR. `R` = initial risk.
- `swing high/low` = pivot with k bars each side (default k=3 on the execution timeframe). `FVG` (bullish) = low[0] > high[2] on 3 consecutive bars; bearish mirror. `MSS` = close beyond most recent opposite swing point. `sweep` = high > level and close < level (bearish) / low < level and close > level (bullish).
- Testability: `Y` = fully codeable from 1-min OHLCV; `Proxy` = needs an OHLCV approximation of a volume-profile/TPO/discretionary concept (e.g., 1-min volume-at-close-price profile, 30-min TPO brackets from 1-min bars); `N` = needs order flow/DOM/tick/options/fundamental data.
- Popularity signals: TradingView counters are quoted as seen on the script page; TV does not label them in the scraped text, so the first number is most likely boosts and the large number most likely uses/views. "n/r" = not reported / not found.
- Claimed performance: "marketing" = self-reported by a vendor/author without independent audit; "backtest (3rd-party)" = independent test; "n/r" = none found.

## Any public mechanical backtests found

Results are listed as reported. Negative results come first because they are the most useful. "Gross vs net" matters: several setups that look positive gross go to zero or below after realistic costs.

**Negative or no-edge results**
1. **Zarattini/Aziz 5-min ORB replicated** (5 markets, 2015–2026, about 2,900 sessions each). Gross reproduces the paper (NQ +0.131R). Net of spread/slippage (NQ 2.5 pts), NQ is +0.002R (t=0.0), SPX −0.081R, Dow −0.081R, DAX −0.038R, FTSE −0.079R. The first candle's directional edge over random is about 0.1R, roughly one round-trip cost. Best out-of-sample result (2021–26) is NQ +0.06R. https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235
2. **London breakout EUR/USD** (1h, 2016–2026; Asian range 00–06 UTC, entries 07–10 UTC, 2:1). −28%, CAGR −3.2%, PF 0.87, 36.3% win, 1,230 trades, max DD −31.4%. https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/london-breakout-eur-usd. GBP/USD 15m version: −60% (https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/london-breakout-gbp-usd).
3. **VWAP 2σ band fade, DAX 15m** (10y, 30-pt TP / 50-pt SL). −98.6%, PF 0.64, 59% win, 8,943 trades, about 358% of position value paid in fees. https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/vwap-band-fade-dax
4. **ICT kill-zone FVG retest, GBP/USD 15m** (NY AM 12–15 UTC, EMA50 filter, 30/60 pips, 10y). −30.3%, PF 0.80, 33.9% win, 1,009 trades, every year negative. https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/ict-kill-zone-fvg-gbp-usd
5. **Previous-day high/low breakout, GBP/USD 1h** (10y). −65.6%, PF 0.76, 2,042 trades, every year negative. https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/previous-day-high-low-breakout-gbp-usd
6. **Order block, GBP/USD 1h**: −15.3%. **MACD+RSI, EUR/USD 1h**: −53.4%. **Bollinger+Stochastic, USD/CAD 1h**: −42.5%. **Three white soldiers, USD/JPY 1h**: −68.6%. **MACD+200EMA, USD/JPY 4h**: −22.1%. All from the Backtrex index at https://backtrex.com/en/backtests.
7. **Guru ORB retest variant** (YouTube 3rd-party test, long history): ES −66%, NQ +14%, gold −50%, euro −97%. A second guru method failed once 10 years of costs were included. In the same video, the simplest ORB beat all guru variants (euro +630%, ES +77%). https://videohighlight.com/v/UFjajYgJBHg (video summary, unverified)
8. **15-min NQ ORB** (systematic implementation of a YouTube "Bull Barbie" tutorial; TP about 1:1, stop on the opposite side). Reported negative over 1,354 trades, 2010–2026. I saw this only in a search-result summary and could not locate the script URL, so treat it as unverified.
9. **Kivanc SuperTrend strategy** (Python port). Gold H4 PF 1.97, but 97% of the profit came from the 2024–26 rally. NZDUSD lost money in every test. https://hackernoon.com/the-truth-about-tradingviews-supertrend-strategy-after-67-years-of-backtesting
10. **Renko backtests (NexusFi, ES/NQ)**: users report more than 50% variance between backtest and live, and "epic fail" in sim. In a Substack Renko-vs-range-bar study, Renko backtests looked better but stayed negative in market replay. https://automatedtradingstrategies.substack.com/p/research-question-what-if-we-use
11. **Gold London/hourly breakout (ProRealCode)**: posted without spread. A commenter tested it "on almost all pairs, does not have an edge." https://www.prorealcode.com/prorealtime-trading-strategies/effective-gold-breakout-strategy
12. **Market Profile 80% rule**: informal TradeStation test about 62% completion; forum sample 75/120 = 62.5%; another user couldn't reproduce even that. https://elitetrader.com/et/threads/trading-with-market-profile.9727/post-598444 and https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/80-percent-rule/
13. **Edgeful power-hour data**: a new HOD/LOD in the last hour happened only about 20% of the time (TSLA, 6/2024–12/2024). This argues against naive power-hour breakouts. https://www.edgeful.com/blog/posts/power-hour-breakout-trading-strategy

**Positive but thin, unaudited or before costs**
14. **ICT FVG + engulfing, NAS100 15m** (10y, 1:3). +37.7% vs buy-and-hold +527%, PF 1.08, 27% win, 852 trades, max DD −23.7%. The edge faded in 2024–26. https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/ict-fair-value-gap-nasdaq
15. **Equal-lows liquidity sweep, US30 1m** (long only, 1:3, 10/2024–10/2026). +23.7%, PF 1.95, 41% win, max DD −3.9%. Only 70 trades. https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/liquidity-sweep-dow-jones
16. **Concretum noise-area intraday momentum, SPY 2007–2024** (authors' own backtest). +1,985% net, 19.6%/yr, Sharpe 1.33. Turnover-sensitive, and I found no independent replication. https://concretumgroup.com/beat-the-market-an-effective-intraday-momentum-strategy-for-sp500-etf-spy/
17. **Market intraday momentum (Gao/Han/Li/Zhou, SPY 1993–2013)**: R² about 0.02; gross 6.3%/yr for last-half-hour timing. https://alphaarchitect.com/2014/08/attention-prop-traders-the-first-half-hour-of-trading-predicts-the-last-half-hour/
18. **Edgeful ES 5-min ORB**: 115 trades, 72% win, PF 1.62 over 6 months, optimized, costs not stated. **Edgeful GC IB algo**: +$105,890 in 12 months on 2 GC, PF 1.94, DD 11.6%, trade count and costs not stated. Both are vendor marketing. https://www.edgeful.com/blog/posts/5-minute-opening-range-breakout-es-strategy and https://www.edgeful.com/blog/posts/gc-trading-strategy-initial-balance-algo
19. **Zarattini/Barbon/Aziz ORB on "Stocks in Play"** (equities, not futures): Sharpe 2.81, +1,600% net 2016–23 (paper). QuantConnect recreation: Sharpe 2.4. https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/
20. **Automated Trading Strategies Substack** (NQ-heavy portfolio, Q1-2025 sim forward test): 113 trades, 67% win, +$84k. Simulated and a short sample. https://automatedtradingstrategies.substack.com/p/q2-2025-forward-test-results
21. **Trading Rush 100-trade manual tests** (30-min FX, 1.5R): MACD about 62%, Donchian 58%, Ichimoku 53%, STC 50% win. Self-reported, not futures. https://it.tradingview.com/script/4gXXEI5Y-Trading-Rush-Signals-Alerts
22. **Holmberg/Lönnbark/Lundström (2013, Finance Research Letters)**: academic test of ORB success rate and profitability; I did not capture the figures. https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v10y2013i1p27-33.html

**Overall reading**: the only independently tested intraday setups here (ORB net of costs, London breakout, VWAP band fade, kill-zone FVG, PDH/PDL breakout) are flat to negative after costs. The positive results are vendor-reported, very short, small-sample, or academic and gross of slippage. In the 2008–2026 harness, treat cost modelling (about 1–2 ticks of slippage per side, plus commissions) as the deciding variable.
