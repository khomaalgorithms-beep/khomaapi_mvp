# Academic & Quant-Research Strategy Catalog (intraday / short-horizon futures)

Scope: academic and quant-research literature (journals, SSRN/arXiv, Quantpedia, CXO, Alpha Architect, Concretum, Unger Academy, Quantified Strategies, Robot Wealth). Target: 1-min OHLCV FirstRate data 2008–Oct 2026, ET, bar-start timestamps; Lucid-approved futures; flat by 16:45 ET (an 18:00→16:45 hold is one session and is allowed). Treasuries may be used as signals only.

Conventions:
- **n/r** = not reported in the source, or not verified by us. We did not fill in numbers from memory when unsure.
- "Rule" gives the source's rule where it is stated. **[adapt]** marks the minimal change needed to make a rule testable under our constraints (instrument mapping or flat-by-16:45). It is not the source's rule.
- Testable column: **Y** = directly testable on 1-min OHLCV with flat-by-16:45. **Proxy** = testable only through an OHLCV proxy, such as a round-number strike for options open interest, a futures substitute for an ETF or stock, or a scheduled-time window instead of a consensus surprise. **N** = needs data we do not have (surprises vs consensus, options OI, order flow, tick/quote) or needs multi-day holds.
- ET times assume US daylight time unless noted. Foreign fixes shift by 1h around DST mismatches and must be computed per date.
- Practitioner sources (Unger, QS, Robot Wealth, Concretum blog) are lower-evidence and are flagged as such.

## Top 15 most credible for intraday futures after costs

The ranking weighs evidence quality (peer review, sample length, cross-asset replication), whether the source accounts for costs, post-publication survival, and fit with 1-min OHLCV and flat-by-16:45.

1. **ACAD-005 Market intraday momentum, equity index futures (Baltussen et al. 2021).** JFE paper across 60+ futures over 46 years with a hedging-flow mechanism. One trade per day in liquid ES/NQ keeps costs low.
2. **ACAD-001 First half-hour → last half-hour (Gao-Han-Li-Zhou 2018).** Foundational JFE result, replicated in 12/16 international markets. A simple single-trade rule.
3. **ACAD-017 Noise-area momentum + VWAP stop + vol sizing (Zarattini-Aziz-Barbon 2024).** One of the few studies reporting net-of-cost results (SR 1.33). Expect decay: an independent OOS re-test gives SR 0.39.
4. **ACAD-007 Market intraday momentum, commodity futures (Baltussen).** Same mechanism, extended to CL/NG/GC/ags. Diversifies the equity version, with settlement-time adjustments.
5. **ACAD-008 Market intraday momentum, currency futures (Baltussen).** Same evidence base. FX futures are cheap to trade relative to the signal.
6. **ACAD-080 NG EIA storage-day premium (Prokopczuk et al. 2021).** Peer-reviewed. Authors report profits after transaction and funding costs. The schedule is fixed and fully testable.
7. **ACAD-068/069/070 Dollar W-pattern at the Tokyo/ECB/London fixes (Krohn-Mueller-Whelan 2024, JF).** Top-journal paper, 21 years, mechanism identified by natural experiments. Gross ~2bp/day is thin, so test on 6E/6J with realistic ticks. (Counted as one slot.)
8. **ACAD-097 FOMC-day premium (Brusa-Savor-Wilson 2020).** Robust across countries and an intraday-holdable day. Few trades, so cost drag is low. Watch for post-2015 decay as with the pre-FOMC drift.
9. **ACAD-098 Macro announcement-day premium (Savor-Wilson 2013; Ai-Bansal 2018).** Long ES on scheduled CPI/NFP/FOMC days. Long-sample evidence in top journals; low turnover.
10. **ACAD-014 Crude EIA-day third half-hour → last half-hour (Wen et al. 2023).** A futures-specific intraday momentum variant tied to a fixed 10:30 Wednesday event.
11. **ACAD-020 5-min ORB (Zarattini-Aziz).** Well-specified and widely tested. An independent replication found the gross edge reproduces but is ≈0 net on CFDs. Futures' lower costs may change that, but treat it as marginal.
12. **ACAD-115/117 LETF and gamma end-of-day momentum (Cheng-Madhavan; Barbon et al. 2022).** The mechanism overlaps with #1. Treat it as a filter on ACAD-005, not a standalone, given the counter-evidence (Lenkey 2024).
13. **ACAD-064/065 Home-currency depreciation in local hours (Ranaldo; Breedon-Ranaldo).** A long-documented FX time-zone seasonality, tradeable on 6E/6J/6B within one Globex session.
14. **ACAD-106 Turn-of-the-month, intraday version (McConnell-Xu 2008).** Among the longest-sample calendar effects. Low turnover; the intraday adaptation must be re-verified.
15. **ACAD-112 ES pinning on expiration days (Golez-Jackwerth 2012).** JFE paper on ES futures specifically. Low frequency, and the round-strike proxy is testable.

## Known failures / negative replications

- **Pre-FOMC drift disappeared after 2015.** Kurov, Wolfe & Gilbert (2021, FRL 40). The 49 bp 24h drift of Lucca-Moench essentially vanished after 2015.
- **Overnight drift (02:00–03:00 ET) is flat 2021–2025** in ES, NQ and YM, per the original authors (Boyarchenko-Larsen-Whelan, Liberty Street Economics 2026). Even pre-2021, CXO notes bid-ask spreads flip its average return and Sharpe negative.
- **"Beat the Market" noise-area momentum decays OOS.** Paz (2026, SSRN 7290621) replicates in-sample but finds an OOS Sharpe of 0.39 vs 1.33.
- **5-min ORB: net ≈ 0.** An MQL5 replication on five index CFDs (https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235) reproduced the gross edge but found net returns near zero. The original paper has no OOS period and ignores spread/slippage.
- **Intraday reversals after large opens lose significance after costs.** Grant, Wolf & Yu (2005, JBF): significance "sharply reduced" after a bid-ask proxy.
- **Intraday technical rules on SPDR: none survive data-snooping correction.** Marshall, Cahan & Cahan (2008, JEF): 0 of 7,846 rules.
- **Intraday FX technical rules: no profits after costs.** Neely & Weller (2003, JIMF); Curcio, Goodhart, Guillaume & Payne (1997, IJFE).
- **London 4pm fix intraday reversal (EUR/USD) fails after costs.** Robot Wealth charts, as cited by FXEmpire: profit per round trip is "tiny".
- **LETF late-day rebalancing effects are economically insignificant.** Ivanov & Lenkey (FEDS 2014-106); Lenkey (2024 survey); Barbon et al. (2022) find LETF effects decreasing over time.
- **Micro NQ, 14 intraday signal families: zero pass walk-forward plus cost.** arXiv 2605.04004 (2026), 2021–2025 sample.
- **Chinese metals: daytime first-half-hour predictability disappeared once night trading began** (Bouri et al.). Intraday momentum is fragile to session-structure changes, which matters because CME Globex hours changed over 2008–2026.
- **Intraday momentum is not pervasive in Asia-Pacific markets** (one APAC study, per search summaries; authors not verified). Li-Sakkas-Urquhart find it in 12/16 developed markets, not all.
- **Corn USDA report effects are "gone in ten minutes".** Lehecka, Wang & Garcia (2014); Adjemian & Irwin (2018). Not exploitable at 1-min resolution after the opening spike.
- **Crude oil barely responds to US macro news at daily frequency.** Kilian & Vega (2011, REStat).
- **Pre-announcement drift exists for only 9 of 20 market-moving releases.** Kurov et al. (2019, JFQA). Lucca-Moench find no pre-announcement equity drift for non-FOMC releases.
- **Fast-alpha short-horizon mean-reversion is unprofitable standalone.** Pagani & Zarattini (2026); useful only as an execution overlay.
- **Classic calendar effects decayed.** The weekend/Monday effect (French 1980; Smirlock-Starks 1986) and Harris (1986) intraday weekday patterns weakened materially in later samples, and Caporale-Plastun note the weekend effect has "become less important". Re-test on 2008–2026 before trusting any of them.
