# LucidFlex 50K strategy search — results (2026-10-11)

## What was done
- Catalog: 615 entries from 3 research passes (catalog/: academic 160, classic/multi-asset 198, practitioner 257).
- Data: FirstRate 1-min unadjusted continuous, 25 Lucid-approved futures + ZN, 2008-01 .. 2026-10-09.
  Roll jumps found INSIDE sessions (00:00 ET) — detected (19/19 vs NQ adjusted file) and back-adjusted.
- Simulator: LucidFlex 50K rules (lucid_sim.py, 12 tests vs Lucid's worked examples). Trade engine verified
  against a bar-by-bar reference (0 mismatches / 1,600 trades). Costs: Lucid commissions + 1 tick/side,
  stop-first on ambiguous bars, gap-through stop fills, targets need a 1-tick trade-through.
- Search: 26,883 trials (27 families x params x 25 markets). Protocol pre-registered: discover 2008-19,
  validate 2020-23 (Holm 10%), holdout 2024-26 locked until PREREGISTRATION.md was written.

## Search outcome
- Forward fold: 60 first-gate survivors -> 0 pass Holm. Grain IB breakouts (best in-sample, t 3-4) flipped
  negative out of sample (ZS t -5.1). ORB, VWAP fade, Silver Bullet, PDH/PDL sweep, gaps, EMA/Supertrend,
  squeeze, 80% rule, Judas/PO3, FX fix, EIA, turn-of-month, overnight drift: all fail after costs.
- Reverse fold: one family stands out — NQ intraday momentum: 100% of 256 variants positive 2020-23
  (median +0.075R) vs 18% in 2008-19. ES weaker, YM/RTY/NKD no.

## Verdict
Not "push it out and sell" ready as a guaranteed-performance product. It is the only rule out of 26,883 trials
that survived honest testing, it is modest (PF ~1.2-1.3), regime-dependent (did not work 2008-2019), and flat
for the last ~9-12 months. Sell-able only with honest, scenario-based expectations, after a live/demo
confirmation that the edge has resumed.

## Speed-up study (speed_study.py, speed_rules.py) — keep ~49% pass, pass much faster
- Stacking more NQ momentum entries (10:00/11:00/12:00/noise-area) does NOT help: they are 0.4-0.6 correlated,
  so it is equivalent to sizing up (all 5 streams: 20% pass in 16d). (An earlier version of this test had a
  look-ahead/overlap bug that flattered stacking; fixed: same-day trades sized from start-of-day state,
  simultaneous worst excursions assumed.)
- What works is exploiting Lucid's rules: (1) pass take-profit = a limit at exactly the $ still needed to reach
  $53,000 with best-day <= 50%; (2) a $1,450 daily take-profit (just under the 50% consistency limit).
  Conservative: if the worst excursion came before the best and would breach, the breach wins.
| config | 2020-23 (design) | 2024-26 (check) | full 2020-26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $450 hold-to-close (old) | 53% / 52d | 35% / 37d | 46% / 46d |
| $600 + rule exits | 55% / 20d | 39% / 19d | 49% / 20d |
| **$800 + rule exits (recommended)** | **52% / 13d** | **40% / 15d** | **47% / 14d (75th 21d)** |
| $1,000 + rule exits | 41% / 9d | 39% / 10d | 40% / 9d |
(pass rate / median trading days to pass, rolling starts, 120-day window)
2026 YTD every config is ~20% pass — the edge has been flat this year; rule exits only change speed.

## Stress test of the final NQ fast-pass logic (stress.py; exact minute replay)
Baseline (Lucid $1.00 RT/MNQ, 1 tick/side): PF 1.39, $29/MNQ/trade, pass 41%, median 6d.
Luck baseline corrected: RANDOM-DIRECTION placebo (same timing/stops/exits) passes ~28% -> true edge ~+13 pts
(the earlier "zero-edge shift" baseline of ~20% was too generous).
- Costs: $1-$3 RT x 1-10 ticks/side: PF 1.39 -> 1.20, pass 41% -> 34%; worst ($3 RT + 8 ticks + 4-tick TP
  trade-through) PF 1.23, pass 35%, 6d. TP trade-through 2-4 ticks: no effect.
- Execution: 1 min late no effect; 5 min late PF 1.29 / 37%; 15 min PF 1.21 / 36%. Missing 10/30/50% of signals:
  PF unchanged (1.36-1.48), pass 39-46%, slower (7/9/12d). Realistic-bad combo ($2 RT + 4 ticks + 30% missed +
  1 min late): PF 1.30, pass 33%, 10d, edge +8 over luck.
- Params: stop 0.2-0.5 ATR, trend 10/50d, first signal 10:15, risk $800-1,200, daily TP $1,000-1,500 all PF 1.22-1.41,
  pass 36-46%. 10:45 first signal / no counter-trend leg are weaker (PF 1.22).
- Placebos: random direction PF 0.93-1.02 (no edge) -> direction is the edge. Random entry 0-3h after the signal:
  PF 1.16-1.24 (timing matters). Remove top 5% best trades -> PF 0.99; top 10% -> 0.76: profit = fat right tail.
- Years: PF > 1 in 16/19 years; 2009 0.82, 2010/2013 ~1.0, 2026 YTD 1.00 (pass 28% = luck). 2008-19 PF 1.22.
- Lucid rule changes: consistency 40% (TP adapted) 41%; optional DLL $800/$1,200 no harm.
- Block-bootstrap MC (400 histories): pass 5th-95th pct 33-49%, median days 5-7, PF 1.20-1.57, P(PF<1) 0%.

## Improvement search: no-trade days, news, cool-offs (filter_study.py, regime_filter.py, fomc_exit.py)
Rule (pre-registered): skip a day type only if its trades lose in EACH of 2008-19 / 2020-23 / 2024-26, do worse than
kept trades in each, n>=20 per period, pooled p<0.05. Official event dates: data/events.csv (Fed + BLS, 835 events).
- Calendar (weekday, OPEX, quad witching, holidays, month start/end): none pass.
- Market state known before 10:30 (08:30 price shock, vol regime, overnight move, chop, after trend/inside/NR7 day):
  none pass. ("after inside day" and "low vol" look good pooled but flip sign in one period -> rejected.)
- Cool-off / equity-curve: next-trade R has ~0 correlation with the last 5-40 trades; all pause rules rejected.
- News: CPI/NFP/PPI days are BETTER than average (NFP +0.22/+0.25/+0.50R) -> keep trading them.
  FOMC days lose in 2 of 3 periods (-0.12, -0.27R; 2020-23 +0.16 < kept), p=0.054 -> not robust. Flattening 1 min
  before the statement: +0.004R (t 0.03) -> no help. Skipping FOMC changes Lucid pass by -0.7..+1.1 pts (noise).
Conclusion: no filter robustly improves the logic; FOMC-skip is optional risk hygiene (~8 days/yr), not a booster.
